<THEOREM_STATEMENT id="1">
Proper proximality is understood in the sense introduced by Ding--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Peterson.

Let \(\Gamma=(\V,\E)\) be a finite simplicial graph with \(|\V|\geq 3\). A finite graph \(\Gamma=(\V,\E)\) is called \emph{irreducible} if it is not a non-trivial join: equivalently, there is no non-empty proper subset \(S\subset \V\) such that every vertex of \(S\) is adjacent to every vertex of \(\V\setminus S\). Equivalently, the complement graph \(\Gamma^c\) is connected.

Let \((M_v,\tau_v)\) be a separable tracial von Neumann algebra for every \(v\in \V\). For \(U\subset \V\), let \(\Gamma_U\) be the induced subgraph and let
\[
(M_U,\tau_U)=\mathop{\star}_{v\in \Gamma_U}(M_v,\tau_v)
\]
denote the corresponding graph product von Neumann algebra. Put \(M_\varnothing=\C\). The canonical trace-preserving conditional expectation is denoted
\[
E_U:M\to M_U.
\]
Write
\[
\Lk(v)=\{w\in \V: (v,w)\in \E\},
\qquad
\St(v)=\Lk(v)\cup\{v\}.
\]

Assume:
\[
\text{(a) }\Gamma\text{ is irreducible, i.e. not a non-trivial join;}
\]
\[
\text{(b) every }M_v\text{ contains a trace-zero unitary.}
\]
Let
\[
(M,\tau)=\mathop{\star}_{v\in\Gamma}(M_v,\tau_v)
\]
be the tracial graph product von Neumann algebra.

Statement:
\[
M\text{ is properly proximal.}
\]
</THEOREM_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="1">
Proper proximality for finite von Neumann algebras is in the sense introduced by Ding--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Peterson.

Let \(M\) be a finite von Neumann algebra. If \(N\subset M\) is a von Neumann subalgebra with expectation, let \(X_N\) denote the \(M\)-boundary piece associated with \(N\), i.e. the boundary piece generated by the Jones projection
\[
e_N:L^2(M)\to L^2(N).
\]
The small-at-infinity operator system is denoted
\[
S_{X_N}(M).
\]

Statement:
\(M\) is properly proximal relative to \(X_N\) exactly when there is no \(M\)-central state on \(S_{X_N}(M)\) whose restriction to \(M\) is normal.
</PROPOSITION_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="1">
This is by Ding--Kunnawalkam Elayavalli--Peterson, Theorems \(6.2\) and \(8.5\).

External citations: C. Ding, S. Kunnawalkam Elayavalli and J. Peterson, \emph{Properly proximal von Neumann algebras}, Duke Math. J. \(\mathbf{172}\) (2023), no. \(15\), 2821--2894, Theorems \(6.2\) and \(8.5\).
</PROPOSITION_PROOF>

\[
(M,\tau)=\mathop{\star}_{v\in\Gamma}(M_v,\tau_v)
\]
be the tracial graph product von Neumann algebra.

Statement:
For every \(v\in\V\), the graph product \(M\) is properly proximal relative to the boundary piece \(X_{M_{\Lk(v)}}\).
</PROPOSITION_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="7">
Apply Proposition \(4\) to
\[
M
=
M_{\St(v)}
\freeprod_{M_{\Lk(v)}}
M_{\V\setminus\{v\}},
\]
using the decomposition of Proposition \(5\) and the unitaries supplied by Proposition \(6\).
</PROPOSITION_PROOF>

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="8">
Let \(\Gamma\) be finite, irreducible, and \(|\V|\geq 3\). Assume every \(M_s\), \(s\in\V\), contains a trace-zero unitary. Let
\[
(M,\tau)=\mathop{\star}_{v\in\Gamma}(M_v,\tau_v)
\]
be the tracial graph product von Neumann algebra.

Statement:
\[
M\text{ has no amenable direct summand.}
\]
</PROPOSITION_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="8">
If \(pM\) were an amenable direct summand, Connes' amenability criterion would give an \(M\)-central state on \(\B(L^2(pM))\), hence by extension an \(M\)-central state on every \(S_{X_{M_{\Lk(v)}}}(M)\) normal on \(M\), contradicting relative proper proximality from Proposition \(7\).

External citations: Connes' amenability criterion.
</PROPOSITION_PROOF>
<DEPS id="8">7</DEPS>

<THEOREM_PROOF id="1">
For every vertex \(v\), Proposition \(7\) shows that \(M\) is properly proximal relative to the boundary piece \(X_{M_{\Lk(v)}}\).

Proposition \(8\) gives that \(M\) has no amenable direct summand.

By Proposition \(3\),
\[
\bigwedge_{v\in\V} q_{\Lk(v)}=q_{\K}.
\]
Thus the finite family of boundary pieces \(\{X_{M_{\Lk(v)}}:v\in\V\}\) satisfies the hypotheses of the upgrading Proposition \(2\). Consequently \(M\) is properly proximal.
</THEOREM_PROOF>
